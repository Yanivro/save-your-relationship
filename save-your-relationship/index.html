The trap

The harder you try, the worse she feels.

You've tried. Date nights, promises to do better, working harder. And she's still pulling away.

You wanted to show her you're a good husband. A great provider. That's not what she needs right now. She needs you to learn how to see her.

So the effort backfires. She doesn't feel loved. She feels managed. Trying harder at the wrong thing convinces her you still don't get it.

Nobody feels loved because they were impressed. They feel loved because they were understood.
